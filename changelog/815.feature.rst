Added :class:`~pluggy.HookCallPlan`, created with :meth:`pluggy.PluginManager.plan_hook_call`, which freezes the participating implementations and their call order for one hook call ahead of execution. The plan is queried without calling plugin callbacks or changing registrations, refuses to execute after the selected plugins, their ordering or the hook's ``firstresult`` status changed, and never picks up plugins registered after it was created.
